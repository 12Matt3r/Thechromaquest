# 🌌 ChromaShift Anomaly System

## 🧠 Shared Dreamscape Memory

The ChromaShift Anomaly System creates a **persistent, shared dreamscape** where discoveries made by one player can influence the experiences of others. This system seamlessly integrates with the existing surreal experience without disrupting the core gameplay.

## ✨ How It Works

### 1. **Anomaly Discovery**
- The game automatically detects interesting/anomalous scenes you encounter
- Surreal elements, impossible geometries, and consciousness-bending moments get logged
- About 40% of highly anomalous scenes are automatically added to the shared database

### 2. **Player Injection** 
- Click the **"📋 Log Anomaly"** button in the command interface
- Upload images (like your baby dolphin photo) and write descriptions
- Your creation becomes part of the shared dreamscape for other players

### 3. **Dream Influence**
- As you play, the system randomly checks for relevant anomalies
- When the dream logic finds your anomaly relevant (30% chance), it influences the scene
- Your baby dolphins might appear in meat coolers, floating through impossible geometries
- The more encounters, the stronger the anomaly becomes (up to 100% influence)

### 4. **Shared Experience**
- Other players may encounter your injected anomalies in their dreams
- Your discoveries help shape the collective unconscious of the dreamscape
- The system creates a growing library of shared surreal experiences

## 🎮 Integration Features

### **Seamless UI**
- Anomaly feed appears in bottom-right corner (non-intrusive)
- Modal popup for logging new anomalies
- Styled to match ChromaShift's neon-pink and electric-cyan aesthetic

### **Smart Detection**
Keywords that trigger automatic anomaly logging:
- Liquid, melting, floating, whisper, echo, mirror, shadow
- Distorted, pulsing, shimmering, glowing, transcendent
- Impossible, unreal, dream, consciousness, reality
- Special triggers: "baby dolphin", "impossible geometry", "temporal"

### **Influence System**
- Each anomaly has an influence strength (10-90% initially)
- Player-injected anomalies start with 60-90% influence
- Each encounter increases influence by 2%
- Maximum influence caps at 100%

### **Contextual Injection**
- System adds relevant anomaly context to narrative generation
- Example: "The air shimmers with memories of baby dolphins in the meat cooler"
- Dream echoes from other players' experiences subtly influence scenes
